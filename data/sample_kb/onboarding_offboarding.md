# Lumio Logistics Employee Onboarding and Offboarding Guide

## New Employee Accounts

HR submits the new hire request at least five working days before the start date. IT then creates the company email account, adds the employee to the standard groups for their department, and prepares a laptop. On the first day the employee receives a temporary password, must change it at first sign-in to one of at least 14 characters, and must register the company authenticator app for MFA before accessing email or VPN.

## Laptop Provisioning

Office staff receive a standard 14-inch business laptop; engineering and data teams may receive a higher specification model with manager approval. Warehouse supervisors receive a rugged tablet in addition to a laptop. All devices are encrypted, enrolled in device management, and preloaded with the LumioConnect VPN client, Microsoft 365, and endpoint security. Laptops are collected from the IT desk on the second floor of the head office or shipped to remote employees.

## Requesting Access to Systems

Access to business systems such as the warehouse management system, the transport management system, finance tools, or shared drives is requested through the Service Desk portal. Every request needs the system name, the role needed, and manager approval. Access to Confidential or Restricted data also needs approval from the data owner. Standard requests are completed within two working days.

## Changing Roles

When an employee moves to a new team, the new manager requests the new access and the previous manager confirms which old access should be removed. Access that is no longer needed for the new role is removed within five working days of the move.

## Leaving the Company

HR notifies IT of the last working day. On that day at 18:00 the employee's accounts are disabled, MFA registrations are removed, and the device is remotely locked. The employee must return the laptop, tablet, badge, chargers, and any security keys to their manager or the IT desk before leaving. Remote employees receive a prepaid return box. Mailbox contents are kept for 90 days and may be forwarded to the manager on request. Employees must not copy company data to personal devices or accounts before leaving.
