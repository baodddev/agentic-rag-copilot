# Lumio Logistics Workplace Technology FAQ

## How do I contact the IT Service Desk?

The Service Desk is open Monday to Friday, 07:00 to 19:00. Call extension 4357 from a desk phone, open a ticket in the Service Desk portal, or email itsupport@lumio.example. Outside these hours, P1 issues such as a company-wide outage or a security incident go to the on-call engineer through the same extension, which forwards automatically. Include your name, asset tag, and a screenshot of any error in every ticket.

## How do I connect to office Wi-Fi?

Company laptops connect automatically to the "Lumio-Corp" network using their device certificate; no password is needed. Personal phones and visitors use "Lumio-Guest", which only reaches the internet. The guest password changes every Monday and is shown at reception. Do not connect company laptops to the guest network.

## How do I set up email on my phone?

Install the Microsoft Outlook app and sign in with your company email. You will be asked to enroll the phone in device management, which requires a screen lock and allows IT to remove company data, but not your personal photos or apps, if the phone is lost. Built-in mail apps are not supported.

## How do I add a printer?

Office printers use secure print. Send the document to the "Lumio-SecurePrint" queue, which is installed automatically on company laptops, then tap your badge on any office printer to release it. Jobs that are not released are deleted after 24 hours. Report paper jams or toner problems to reception, and printing errors on your laptop to the Service Desk.

## My warehouse scanner will not sync. What should I do?

First place the scanner in its charging cradle for one minute and confirm the Wi-Fi icon is shown. Then restart the scanner by holding the power button for ten seconds. If orders still do not appear, tell the shift supervisor, who opens a P2 ticket if the whole shift is affected or a P3 ticket for a single scanner. Do not factory-reset scanners; they need to be re-enrolled by IT.

## Can I use my own laptop for work?

No. Company data may only be accessed on company-managed laptops. Personal phones may be used for Outlook and Teams after device enrollment. Contractors who bring their own laptops use the virtual desktop service, which IT sets up on request.

## How do I join or host video meetings?

Use Microsoft Teams for all internal and customer meetings. Meeting rooms have a Teams console on the table; tap "Join" on the scheduled meeting. If room audio does not work, check that the console is not muted and that the meeting is using "Room audio" rather than your laptop. Report a broken room console to the Service Desk with the room name.
