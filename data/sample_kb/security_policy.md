# Lumio Logistics Information Security Policy

## Reporting Phishing and Suspicious Email

Do not click links, open attachments, or reply to an email that asks for passwords, MFA codes, payment changes, or urgent gift card purchases. Use the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook, which sends the message to the Security team and removes it from your inbox. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, change your password immediately through the self-service identity portal and call the IT Service Desk at extension 4357 so Security can review your account. Reporting quickly is never punished; a late report causes far more damage.

## Data Classification

Lumio Logistics data has four classes. Public: approved marketing material and published rates. Internal: procedures, org charts, and general business email. Confidential: customer contracts, shipment manifests, pricing agreements, and employee records. Restricted: payment card data, government ID numbers, and security credentials. Confidential and Restricted data may only be stored in the company SharePoint and approved business systems, never in personal cloud storage, personal email, or messaging apps. Restricted data must not be sent by email without encryption.

## Removable Media and USB Devices

USB storage devices are blocked by default on company laptops. Use company SharePoint or OneDrive links to share files instead. If a business process requires USB storage (for example, loading firmware onto warehouse scanners), the manager must request a time-limited exception through the Service Desk. Never plug in a USB device you found or received from an unknown sender; hand it to IT.

## Public Wi-Fi and Travel

On hotel, airport, or café Wi-Fi, connect to the LumioConnect VPN before opening any company system. Do not use shared or public computers to access company email or files. When travelling abroad, tell the Service Desk at least five working days in advance so sign-ins from the destination country are not blocked.

## Security Incident Reporting

Report suspected malware, unusual account activity, lost badges, or data sent to the wrong recipient to the IT Service Desk immediately, any time of day. Security incidents are handled as P1 or P2 tickets. Do not try to investigate or clean an infected device yourself; disconnect it from the network and wait for instructions.
